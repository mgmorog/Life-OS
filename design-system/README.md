# Life OS

Life OS is one calm place for your days, plans and life areas. Light: sage-tinted paper, white cards on a hairline, ink text and one green. Titles and big numbers are set in a serif (Newsreader), everything else in Figtree. Motion is smooth and quiet, with a small reward when you finish something.

These are the exact tokens from the app's stylesheet (`src/style.css`, v261), generated by `npm run tokens` in the repo so this reference matches the app. In the app, every value below is used through its CSS custom property, as `var(--name)`.

## Principles

- **Calm before clever.** One accent colour, generous paper, no gradients, no bold. Colour means something: green is action and done, `red` is late, `amber` is a heads-up, area colours belong to their life area.
- **Circles, never ovals.** `r-full` only goes on equal-width-and-height elements (ticks, circles, day numbers) or on pills. A day circle that stretches is a bug.
- **One of each.** Nine type sizes, three weights, one 4-point spacing scale, one icon scale, three opacities, three speeds. If a value isn't a token, it's a question.
- **Reach first.** Every tappable thing is at least 44px (`ctl`). Primary actions sit at the bottom of sheets, within thumb reach.
- **Undo over asking.** Deleting one thing happens straight away with Undo. Several things, or something you can't get back, ask first, and the red button says exactly what goes ("Delete 3 tasks").

## Three layers

1. **Primitives**: the raw colours. The `sage-50` … `sage-900` ramp, `paper`, `ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`, three line greys, `red` and `amber`, the life area colours.
2. **Roles**: what a colour is for. `action`, `action-text`, `done`, `selected`, `overdue`, `surface`, `surface-raised`, `text`, `text-muted`, `text-faint`, `border`, `focus`. Components never use primitives directly.
3. **Components**: one token per component decision, so restyling is one line.
   - Cards: `card-bg`, `card-edge`, `card-radius`, `card-pad`, `card-title-*` (headers inside a card are row text at medium weight; labels outside stay small grey).
   - Buttons: three styles (solid `btn-primary-*` once per sheet, soft `btn-tonal-*` on pages, text links) in two heights, `btn-h` 44 and `btn-h-lg` 52; small in-row buttons `btn-h-sm` 36. Header links use `link-quiet`; green `link` means an action.
   - Chips `chip-h` 36 with `chip-bg`, no border; segmented controls `seg-track`, `seg-fg`, `seg-on-fg`.
   - Fields and search: `field-h` 48, `field-bg`, `field-radius` 14, `field-edge`; placeholders `placeholder`.
   - Navigation: `tab-on-bg` pill behind the active tab, `fab-size` 52 with `fab-shadow`, `back-fg`, frosted `bar-bg` with `bar-edge`.
   - Toasts `toast-*` and menus `menu-shadow`: light, on a hairline.
   - Rows: `row-h`, `row-lead` 24, `row-gap` 12, `divider` (dividers start at the text). Finished items fade to `done-opacity` and are struck through.
   - Also `circle-*`, `tick-ring`, `prio-ring`, `track-bg`.

The palette chosen in Settings (Sage, Ocean, Plum, Clay, Slate or a custom colour) repaints the whole sage ramp from one colour, so every role and component follows: charts, the calendar heat map and the circles included.

## Colour

Paper (`bg`) is the ground; cards (`card-bg`) sit on a 1px `card-edge` hairline instead of a shadow. Text has three tones: `ink` for titles, `ink-2` for subtitles (5.5:1 on paper), `ink-3` for timestamps and counts. `ink-faint` is for grips and disabled glyphs, never text.

Lines: `line-2` is the hairline between rows, `line` outlines controls, `line-strong` is for chevrons. Unchecked checkboxes use the lighter `tick-ring`.

`accent` (`sage-600`) is the one action colour: the + button, checked ticks, the selected day, a sheet's main button. Page buttons, selected chips and circles are soft (`selected`, `sage-100`) with `accent-deep` text.

`red` is only for late and overdue. `amber` is for heads-up: due soon, over capacity. Each has a soft fill.

Each life area has a colour at one even lightness (`a-health`, `a-home`…) and a soft fill (`a-health-soft`…). They appear in charts and on habits you colour; they never replace `accent` for actions. Trips use `a-travel`.

## Type

Serif only for page titles and big numbers; Figtree for everything you read and tap. Sentence case everywhere.

Nine sizes: `fs-micro` 11, `fs-small` 13, `fs-label` 14, `fs-row` 15, `fs-headline` 17, `fs-title` 20, `fs-xl` 24, `fs-page` 30, `fs-display` 44. Text fields alone use `fs-input` 16 so iPhone never zooms in.

Three weights: `fw-regular` 400 for text, `fw-medium` 500 for row titles, labels and buttons, `fw-strong` 600 only for numbers and what is selected.

Section labels are `fs-small` in `ink-2`, and every section starts `gap-section` (24) below what came before.

Line height: 1.1 for titles, 1.4 for UI, 1.5 for anything you read.

## Space and shape

Spacing is a 4-point scale: `s1` 4, `s2` 8, `s3` 12, `s4` 16, `s5` 20, `s6` 24, `s7` 32. Cards, rows and sheets all pad with `pad` (16). The Density setting scales the scale through one `density` value (1, or 0.875 when compact).

Controls are `ctl-sm` 40, `ctl` 44 and `ctl-lg` 56; rows are `row` 52, or `row-2` 64 with a second line. Progress bars are `track` 4.

Corners are concentric: `r-sheet` 28 for sheets, `r-card` 20 for cards, `r-row` 14 for rows and fields inside cards, `r-inner` 10 for small things inside rows, `r-micro` 6 for bars. Chips are always fully round.

## Depth, layers and states

Cards rest on a hairline (`e-raised`). Menus and drag ghosts float (`e-floating`); sheets sit above a light scrim (`e-overlay`).

Layers, bottom to top: `z-base`, `z-raised`, `z-sticky`, `z-bar`, `z-sheet`, `z-toast`, `z-overlay`. Pick one; don't invent numbers.

Charts use `chart-1`…`chart-6` (the accent first, then area colours) and `chart-other`. Bar charts use `chart-bar` with this week in `chart-now`, no axis, and the count above each bar.

States: soft press `press` (0.975) on buttons and chips, `press-row` (0.985) on rows; three opacities: `o-faint` .35 (hints, ghosts), `o-muted` .6 (disabled, finished), `o-strong` .85; focus is a `focus-w` (2px) accent ring.

## Motion

Three speeds (`dur-fast` .18s, `dur-base` .3s, `dur-slow` .5s) and four curves (`ease`, a calm `spring` that overshoots 20%, `soft`, `exit`), read by the stylesheet and by scripted animations alike. Same kind of action, same speed.

Breakpoints: compact ≤380, tablet 700, desktop 900, wide 1100 (`BP` in the code). With Reduce Motion on, nothing travels: changes crossfade so progress still shows.

## Iconography

Outline icons on a 24px grid, round caps and joins, one scale: `icon-xs` 14, `icon-sm` 16, `icon-md` 20, `icon-lg` 24, `icon-xl` 28. Rows use `icon-lg`; module tiles and pinned circles use `icon-xl`. Icons sit plainly in rows, in `ink-2`, without tinted backgrounds. The checkmark is always 2.8. Icon-only buttons always carry a name.

## Components

Button, Chip, Row, Card, Sheet header, Segmented control, Tick, Toast and Empty state are shown in Components, each with its rules. They are drawn here with the tokens; the real ones live in `src/core.js` and `src/style.css`.
